# Transcribe TR

## User

Turkish-speaking editors and production teams preparing transcripts and subtitles.

## Problem

Transcription, timing, cleanup, and subtitle export create repetitive manual work.

## Promise

A privacy-aware Turkish transcription workflow designed for editors.
