# CutMate

## User

Premiere Pro editors who want AI assistance without giving an autonomous system uncontrolled
access to their projects.

## Problem

AI tools often sit outside the editing environment or act without enough visibility and safety.

## Promise

A controlled desktop and Premiere bridge that understands context, proposes actions, and
executes only safe, explicit commands.

## Current stage

`VALIDATING`

## Known direction

- Tauri desktop shell
- TypeScript/React interface
- local bridge
- Premiere UXP integration
- safe marker and sequence actions
- learning and development modules

## Next proof

Create a duplicate sequence and a verified Premiere marker through the controlled bridge,
with visible confirmation and failure handling.
