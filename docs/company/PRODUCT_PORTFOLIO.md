## Portfolio rules

A product remains active only when it has:

- a named user,
- a real problem,
- a clear promise,
- a working or testable proof,
- an owner,
- a next decision.

No new product becomes P1 while another P1 has no defined next proof.

## Stage meanings

- `PROPOSED`: Idea is documented but not tested.
- `VALIDATING`: Building or measuring the smallest proof.
- `ACTIVE`: Publicly operated and maintained.
- `PAUSED`: Intentionally stopped with a review date.
- `RETIRED`: No longer supported or promoted.
