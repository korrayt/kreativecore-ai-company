# Kreative Core Company OS

## Purpose

Kreative Core builds tools and systems that make creative production simpler, faster, more
private, and more accessible.

The Company OS keeps strategy, products, operations, quality, security, marketing, finance,
and releases connected without turning the company into bureaucracy.

## Operating principles

1. **Creator first:** Start with a real creative workflow problem.
2. **Simple outside, strong inside:** Complexity belongs in the system, not on the user.
3. **Privacy by design:** Prefer local processing and minimal data collection.
4. **Proof before scale:** Validate a working outcome before expanding scope.
5. **One owner:** Every active outcome has one accountable owner.
6. **Small complete steps:** Ship coherent increments, not scattered unfinished work.
7. **Truthful communication:** Never market what is not verified.
8. **Reversible decisions:** Keep early decisions easy to change.
9. **Security is product quality:** Credentials and private data never enter Git.
10. **Finish visibly:** Every task ends with an output, validation, and next decision.

## Company rhythm

### Weekly

- Review active products.
- Review blocked work.
- Review top risks.
- Choose the three most important outcomes.
- Close, defer, or reassign stale work.

### Monthly

- Review portfolio focus.
- Review release readiness.
- Review spending assumptions.
- Review distribution and audience learning.
- Update roadmap and risk register.

### Per release

- Confirm product promise.
- Run technical and content checks.
- Confirm licensing and third-party obligations.
- Verify public claims.
- Prepare rollback and support path.

## Decision states

- `PROPOSED`
- `VALIDATING`
- `APPROVED`
- `ACTIVE`
- `BLOCKED`
- `PAUSED`
- `RETIRED`

## Definition of done

An outcome is complete when:

- the intended result exists,
- required checks pass,
- evidence is linked,
- risks are recorded,
- the owner confirms the result,
- the next decision is clear.
